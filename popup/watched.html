Youtube Hider

Marked as watched

Videos you marked with the Watched button on YouTube. They are hidden like watched videos, following the Hide Watched Videos settings. Remove one to bring it back. Up to 3,000 videos are kept: past that, the oldest mark is dropped.

Your marks sync automatically to every computer signed into the same Chrome profile, so there is no need to export and import them between devices. Use Export to keep a backup or to move them to a different profile.

Sync paused: the channel lists need the space shared across devices. Your marked videos are saved in this browser, and syncing resumes once they fit again
