Support YouTube Hider

YouTube Hider is free, open source, and has no ads and no tracking. It also aims at a moving target: YouTube reshapes its page markup without warning, and a change nobody announces can quietly break a filter that worked yesterday.

Keeping it working means hunting those changes down one selector at a time, in someone's spare time. Any of these three helps.

Buy me a coffee A one-off contribution on Ko-fi. No account and no subscription needed. Contribute on GitHub Send a pull request, or report a broken filter with the page it happened on. Leave a review A rating on the Chrome Web Store costs nothing and helps other people find the extension.
