YouTube Hider helps you clean up YouTube by hiding what you don't want to see.
Automatically remove watched videos, filter by views or upload date, and eliminate Shorts, Mixes, Playlists and live streams: all with simple, customizable controls that follow you on every device.

✔ Hide watched videos based on your chosen progress threshold
✔ Mark any video as watched with one click, even when YouTube shows no progress bar or you watched it on another account
✔ Manage every list on one page, built for thousands of channels and words: paste many at once, search, select in bulk, undo, export and import
✔ Filter videos by view count: too few, too many, or both
✔ Hide by upload date: remove videos too new, too old, or both
✔ Remove Shorts, Mixes, Playlists and live streams
✔ Whitelist channels or title words to exempt them from every filter
✔ Blacklist channels or title words to always hide them
✔ Choose to fully hide filtered content or dim it with an overlay showing the filter reason
✔ Easy Mode for quick toggles, Advanced Mode for per-page control
✔ Settings button built right into YouTube's header for instant access on any page
✔ Everything syncs automatically across your devices: settings, whitelists, blacklists and marked videos, with no export needed

Works on Home, Channel, Subscriptions, Search and related video pages.
Fully open source, lightweight, and data-free: sync goes through your own Chrome account, never through external servers.
