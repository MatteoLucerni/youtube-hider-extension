Privacy Policy

YouTube Hider has no servers, no analytics and no ads. Everything it knows about you stays in your browser.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy describes how the YouTube Hider browser extension handles data, in every browser it is available for, including Microsoft Edge and Google Chrome. It also covers this website, youtubehider.com. The extension is developed by Matteo Lucerni and its full source code is public on GitHub, so every statement below can be checked against the code.

In short

  • The extension does not send any data to the developer or to any third party.
  • It has no servers, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no user accounts.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer your data, and no person reads it.
  • It only runs on youtube.com and m.youtube.com, and has no access to any other website.

What the extension reads on YouTube

To decide which videos to hide or dim, the extension reads the video cards shown on YouTube pages: title, channel, view count, upload date, the watch progress bar, and whether a card is a Short, a Mix, a playlist or a live stream. It also reads the page data YouTube loads into the page, to know which channel a video belongs to and whether YouTube labels it as music.

This happens entirely inside your browser, while the page is open. None of it is recorded, except what is listed in the next section. The extension does not keep a history of the pages or videos you visit.

What the extension stores

  • Your settings: which filters are on and their thresholds.
  • Your lists: the channels on your Channel Whitelist and Channel Blacklist, and the words on your Title Whitelist and Title Blacklist.
  • Videos you mark as watched: for each video you mark with the Watched button, its YouTube video ID and the date you marked it, plus its title and channel name so the Manage page can show them. Up to 3,000 videos are kept.
  • Small technical records the extension needs to keep these lists in step between your devices.

Everything is saved in your browser's storage for extensions. You can see, change, export and delete all of it from the extension's settings and its Manage page.

How this data is used

The data above is used for one purpose only: to decide which videos to hide or dim on YouTube, following the settings and lists you chose, and to show you those settings and lists so you can manage them. It is never used for advertising, profiling, analytics, creditworthiness or lending, or any purpose unrelated to that feature.

Who receives your data

Nobody, apart from the two cases below, both of which are part of how your browser works rather than something the extension sends on its own:

  • Your browser provider, if you use browser sync, as described in the next section.
  • YouTube, which receives the video IDs of your marked videos when the Manage page loads their thumbnails, as described under Network connections.

Sync between your devices

Your settings, your lists and the IDs and dates of the videos you marked as watched are also saved in your browser's sync storage. If you are signed in to your browser with sync turned on, your browser provider (Microsoft for Edge, Google for Chrome) copies them to your other devices signed in to the same account, under that provider's own privacy terms. The developer has no access to this data. The titles and channel names of marked videos are never synced: they stay on the device where you marked the video. If sync is turned off, nothing leaves your device.

Network connections

The extension makes no requests to the developer's servers. The only connections it causes are these:

  • The Manage page shows the thumbnails of your marked videos, which your browser loads from YouTube's image servers (i.ytimg.com), the same way YouTube itself loads them.
  • When the extension is first installed, it opens the welcome page on this website.
  • When the extension is removed, your browser opens an optional feedback form hosted on Google Forms. The extension attaches no data to it; anything you choose to type there is handled by Google Forms.

Removing your data

Removing a channel, a word or a marked video in the extension deletes it. Removing the extension makes your browser delete the data it stored on that device. Data synced by your browser can be managed or cleared from your browser's sync settings.

This website

youtubehider.com is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and its fonts are served from the site itself. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy statement. The homepage loads the extension's public user count and rating from shields.io, and the demo video loads from YouTube only after you click play.

Browser store policies

YouTube Hider complies with the Developer policies for the Microsoft Edge Add-ons store. The use of information by YouTube Hider adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes to this policy

If the way the extension handles data changes, this page will be updated before that version is published, and the date at the top will change with it.

Contact

For any question about this policy, use the feedback form or open an issue on GitHub.
