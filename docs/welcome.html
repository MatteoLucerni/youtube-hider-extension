Getting Started with YouTube Hider

Follow these steps to set up the extension and take control of your YouTube experience.

How to use

  1. 1

    Install the extension

    Add YouTube Hider to your browser from the Chrome Web Store. It works on Chrome and all Chromium-based browsers (Edge, Brave, Opera, etc.).

  2. 2

    Pin the extension

    Click the puzzle piece icon in your toolbar and pin YouTube Hider so it's always visible.

  3. 3

    Open settings

    Click the YouTube Hider icon to open the settings popup. Toggle features on and off, adjust thresholds, and customize everything to your liking.

  4. 4

    Use the header button on YouTube

    On desktop, you'll see a small gear button right in YouTube's own header, next to the Create button. Click it for instant access to your full settings without leaving the page.

TIP

Simple Mode vs Advanced Mode

The extension starts in Simple Mode, simple toggles to turn features on or off. When you're ready for more control, turn on Advanced Controls in Extra Settings to switch to Advanced Mode and fine-tune which YouTube pages each filter applies to.

Features

Hide Watched Videos

Already seen a video? It disappears automatically based on how much you've watched. No progress bar on it? Hover it and click Watched to hide it anyway.

Hide Shorts, Mixes, Playlists & Lives

Remove Shorts, Mix playlists, regular Playlists and live streams from your feed, search results and related sidebar.

Filter by Views

Set a minimum view count, a maximum one, or both, and videos outside the range are hidden automatically.

Header Settings Button

A button built right into YouTube's own header (desktop) opens your full settings instantly, on every page.

Upload Date Filter

Hide videos that are too new or too old. Set two independent thresholds to keep only content from a specific time window.

Filter Mode: Hide or Dim

Choose whether filtered content disappears or stays visible with a dark overlay and a label showing why it was filtered.

Channel Whitelist

Exempt your favorite channels from every filter. Add them from the filtered overlay, the Subscribe button, or the popup.

Channel Blacklist

The opposite of Channel Whitelist: always hide a channel's videos everywhere. Add it from the Subscribe button, a hover button on any video card, filtered or not, or the popup.

Title Whitelist & Blacklist

The same pair for words: whitelist a word, and every video with it in its title is left alone by every filter, or blacklist one, and every video with it is always hidden, even from a whitelisted channel.

Master Extension Switch

Instantly pause every filter with one switch in the popup header, without losing any of your settings.

Go to Homepage

You can always access settings by clicking the extension icon in your toolbar.
